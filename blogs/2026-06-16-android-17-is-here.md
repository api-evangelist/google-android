Android 17 shifts the platform toward an intelligence system with mandatory large-screen resizability, expanded AppFunctions capabilities for AI agent integration, and enhanced privacy, security, media, and camera features. The release emphasizes adaptive-first development standards and makes Android Open Source Project source code available for examination.
