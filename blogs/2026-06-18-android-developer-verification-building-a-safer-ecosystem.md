Google is rolling out Android developer verification protections starting September 30, 2026 in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand across seven app stores. The initiative introduces new APIs for bulk app registration and CI/CD pipeline integration, with limited distribution accounts for students and hobbyists launching in July 2026.
